# Third-party assets

Everything in this directory is original work or generated in code, with one exception.

## Cameraman

- File: `src/cameramandata.js` (the pixels, base64-encoded 8-bit grey, 256x256)
- Source: MIT Dome, <https://hdl.handle.net/1721.3/195767>
- Rights holder: Massachusetts Institute of Technology
- Licence: **Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0**
  <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/>
- The original TIFF's own description reads: *"This image is distributed by The MathWorks,
  Inc. with permission from the Massachusetts Institute of Technology."*

Attribution is rendered in the page footer. **Keep it there** -- it is a licence condition.

Note that CC BY-NC is more restrictive than this repository's MIT licence: it forbids
commercial use. That restriction attaches to this image only, not to the code. If the page
ever needs an unrestricted licence throughout, drop the Cameraman scene -- the other three
scenes are procedural and carry no conditions.

## Everything else

`onnxruntime-web` in `vendor/` is MIT-licensed (Microsoft); see
`vendor/LICENSE.onnxruntime-web`. Fonts are served by Google Fonts under the SIL Open Font
License. The USAF-1951 target, star field and cratered terrain are generated by
`src/scene.js` and are not derived from any external image.
